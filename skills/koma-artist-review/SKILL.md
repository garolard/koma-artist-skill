# koma-artist-review

Read [KOMA reference](references/koma.md) and [set plan format](references/set-plan.md). Treat the artist as the final decision-maker. Do not claim to have seen images you cannot inspect; distinguish visual judgment from dimension-based inference. Do not upload artwork, submit a set, overwrite originals, or send private artwork to outside services without explicit authorization. If image access or a tool is unavailable, explain the limit and continue with the evidence available. Keep requirements distinct from recommendations.

Review the collection supplied by the artist and create a complete proposed set plan as a Markdown document when a file location is available, or present it in chat if not. Inspect images where possible; identify excluded or missing candidates and explain significant selection, crop and rarity choices. Include the cover, previews, rarity counts, resolution and framing concerns, and concrete next steps. Flag uncertainty and gaps rather than fabricating a complete set. All choices are proposals until approved. Offer the interactive planning skill to refine it.
