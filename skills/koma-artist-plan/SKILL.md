# koma-artist-plan

Read [KOMA reference](references/koma.md) and [set plan format](references/set-plan.md). Treat the artist as the final decision-maker. Do not claim to have seen images you cannot inspect; distinguish visual judgment from dimension-based inference. Do not upload artwork, submit a set, overwrite originals, or send private artwork to outside services without explicit authorization. If image access or a tool is unavailable, explain the limit and continue with the evidence available. Keep requirements distinct from recommendations.

Work with the artist in manageable stages to select panels, decide titles, shapes, framing, rarity, cover and previews. Ask focused questions when artistic choices need their input; offer concrete suggestions based on available evidence. Maintain a shared Markdown set plan, update it as choices are made, track decisions versus proposals, and show remaining gaps and rarity counts. Accept a plan from review as a starting point. Never silently turn a suggestion into an approved choice.
